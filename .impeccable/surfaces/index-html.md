# Surface: salon landing page (index.html / src/App.vue) — v2 replacement world

Mode: Persuade. Audience: US salon-goers on phones; want to see real work, the vibe of the studio, prices, and book fast. Action: book an open slot (or call/text). Proof: real photos & video of nails and the studio, seasonal collections, social reels. Constraints: prices/contact/name/times are samples, labeled; stock media (Unsplash/Pexels) stands in for the salon's own and is listed for replacement. Stack: Vue 3 + Vite + Docker. Seasonal print runs (10 seasons) with switcher retained.

User feedback that triggered v2: Riso flyer felt "thô cứng, xấu"; wants softer, more modern, beautiful; real photos, video frame, slideshow, TikTok/IG/FB/YouTube reels, concise clear ad copy. Built unattended (user asked to build directly). Code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every season is a new issue of the salon's own glossy beauty magazine: a full-bleed photo cover with masthead and cover lines, then soft editorial spreads; refuses the beige-spa template and the stiff flyer.

OWN-WORLD: Glossy soft ground tinted per issue (blush, mint, plum-night…), white rounded "page" surfaces with long soft shadows, frosted gel-glass chrome, pill buttons, photo-led spreads with generous white space; polaroids with tape and die-cut stickers as the issue's playful inserts. Gloock display serif for masthead/headlines, Figtree for UI/body, Caveat only on handwritten tape notes.

STORY: Visitor sees gorgeous real nails instantly (cover slideshow), reads the offer in one cover line, books from the cover; then flips through spreads: the studio (video), the menu, fresh sets, reels, booking, visit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header; full-bleed rounded cover card filling the viewport, real nail photos crossfading with slow zoom; giant masthead wordmark across the top of the cover, issue name; two or three cover lines (headline + one-line pitch) bottom-left on soft scrim with Book / Call; next-open-slot chips; a taped polaroid of the issue's set and stickers bottom-right; seasonal particles drifting.
